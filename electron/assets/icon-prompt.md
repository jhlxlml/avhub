# AVHub 应用图标

由内置图像生成工具生成；透明底、深蓝圆角方块、珊瑚色播放符号。
`avhub-source.png` 保留生成原图，`avhub.png` 为窗口使用的 512px PNG，
`avhub.ico` 为 Windows EXE 使用的 16/24/32/48/64/128/256px 多尺寸图标。

仅重新导出尺寸（需 Pillow）：`python scripts/prepare-app-icon.py`。

## 原始提示词

Create a polished original Windows desktop application icon for AVHub, an offline local video library and player. One single centered app icon, not a presentation or mockup. Transparent canvas outside the icon. Design a large dark midnight-blue rounded-square tile with a distinctive warm coral play symbol in the center, subtly formed by two overlapping rounded video-card shapes and a clean right-pointing play triangle. Modern, restrained, elegant, strong silhouette, crisp shapes, readable at 16 and 32 pixels. Palette: midnight #0b0f16, charcoal navy #182335, warm coral #f3907e, a small brighter coral highlight. Very subtle dimensional refinement, no glossy plastic, no busy cinematic imagery, no letters, no text, no wordmark, no watermark, no tiny details, no film perforations, no surrounding objects. Front-facing square composition, equal small margins, the rounded tile occupies approximately 92 percent of the square canvas; transparent corners and outside area. High quality production-ready application icon.
